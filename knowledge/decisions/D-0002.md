# D-0002 — Trailer 2 is nfx's Blockbench re-creation, under the same ids

**Decision**: The trailer's source is `devtools/art/preview/trailer.bbmodel`, nfx's
re-creation of the generator's trailer in Blockbench (2026-09-11), as saved; his build,
ported to `devtools/art/build.py`, turns it half a turn to the protocol's frame, cuts the
wheel mesh out, wraps cubes into the profile's folders and measures the profile off the
cubes. The generator's OBJ bundle, the swatch atlas and the converter that turned the
bundle into a Blockbench project are gone. The mod id, the vehicle id `trailer:trailer`
and the recipe are unchanged: the new body has the same footprint, axle, coupler and door
hinges to within a tenth of a block, so trailers in a world and crafted chassis carry
over; the version is 2.1.0, past the 2.0.x nfx shipped to his instance.

**Why**: Rusty's call, on nfx's asking. A hand-built project is where a modeller works;
the generator's bundle needed its parts renamed by geometry and a fake texture.

**Alternatives rejected**: *A new id* -- the geometry matches, and nothing is gained by
orphaning existing trailers.

**Links**: `devtools/art/build.py`, the protocol's D-0008 (the towed pose, the gestures).
