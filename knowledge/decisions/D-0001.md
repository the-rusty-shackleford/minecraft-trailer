# D-0001 — Parts named by geometry, not the header; the wheel turned to put its axle across

**Decision**: The pipeline ignores the bundle's 86 group declarations and names the four
parts the protocol selects by their geometry: the tyres are 48-face prisms of material
`tire`, the hubs 36-face prisms of `hub`, the rear doors the two body-material boxes on
the rear face either side of the centreline. The left wheel and hub are cut out into the
wheel mesh about their centre and turned a quarter turn about Y so the axle runs along X.

**Why**: The header's `g` lines precede every face, so the OBJ attaches one group to all
660 faces; the parts are not six faces each, so walking the header in step with the face
blocks fails. Four names are needed and four geometric tests find them without a guess.
The bundle's tyres are discs facing forward -- a wheel that would roll sideways -- and the
protocol spins its wheel mesh about X; turning the extracted wheel once at build time is
the whole fix, and the frame keeps nothing of the wheels.

**Alternatives rejected**: *Recovering all 86 groups by order* -- fails on the merged
face blocks. *Editing the OBJ by hand* -- unreviewable, unrepeatable.

**Links**: `devtools/art/build.py` (`name_parts`, the wheel turn), the profile's `doors`
and `wheels`.
